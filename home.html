Welcome to MESA Charter High School: South Brooklyn

Math, Engineering, and Science Academy (MESA) Charter High School South Brooklyn is a small community high school in Sotuh Brooklyn, New York. MESA was founded in August 2025 to offer an brand new alternative, non-selective, public high school option to the families of South Brooklyn. There's current 130 students.

At MESA, all students will engage and apply the core values of pride, purpose, empathy, and courage

Teachers and staffs engage with one another as well as engage with students to help them reach the core values and create a path to the future.
